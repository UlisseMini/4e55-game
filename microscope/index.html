How it works

You get one neuron at a time from the second-to-last layer (stage3.block1, 1,024 neurons) of the ConvNeXt-SwiGLU “medium” model from the essay Neural networks are already clean. Study its feature visualizations, then classify 8 photos: each is from either the neuron's positive pole (its strongest responses) or its negative pole (its most negative responses). Half are each, so guessing scores 50%.

Difficulty changes which positive photos you see: the 16 strongest of 50,000 ImageNet validation photos, or ones around the 50th or 500th strongest. The negative photos are always the neuron's 4 most negative, because those are the only negative examples the microscope publishes.

Photos are the microscope's crops, centered where the neuron responds most (or most negatively). All images and values come from the essay's public microscope; we didn't run the model.
